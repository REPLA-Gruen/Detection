# Partner report structure for the later Word document

Use the new `OPEN_FIRST.html` after reviewing the complete outputs. For *every* problem use exactly:

A. The problem — ordinary language.
B. Evidence — sourced photos, numerical examples, simple charts and tables.
C. Important observations — why the issue matters, without assigning unproven blame.
D. Required actions — named Swiss role and separate CCBRE task, with a blank decision field.

Place general statistics first, problem chapters in sequence, and one consolidated meeting decision log at the end. Do not paste internal run paths or software names into the Swiss-facing body. Keep the technical files in the reproducibility appendix. Do not claim missing boxes are errors until an annotation owner reviews the source photograph.
